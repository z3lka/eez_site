The same thinking, at every scale.

Elif Ebrar Zelka has counted pedestrians across a district, designed housing, coordinated the making of furniture, directed an immersive room and designed a screen. The job title changes each time. The method does not: observe, question, draw, make, manage.

Urban95, Zümrütevler Square

Maltepe, Istanbul · Superpool with NACTO-GDCI · team member · interim build October 2019

Urban95 asks one question: if you could experience the city from 95 cm, the height of a three-year-old, what would you change? In Zümrütevler the answer was tested before it was built. Paint, planters and movable furniture stood in for construction, and the square was measured before and after.

The counts were taken by hand: people by age, gender and activity, pedestrians against cars, and maps of where children actually played. They turned a temporary trial into the case for a permanent square, and 1,075 m² of asphalt went from cars to people.

Figures as reported in the Zümrütevler Square interim implementation report, Superpool for the Bernard van Leer Foundation. Project page at Superpool.

HOP

Istanbul · Superpool, Istanbul Metropolitan Municipality, Istanbul Volunteers, Bernard van Leer Foundation · on-site coordination

HOP set out to show that any empty corner of the city can become a playground. The team prepared the sites and handed children cardboard, rope, fabric and tape; a patch of pavement became tunnels and tents within the hour. The work was on the ground, building alongside them.

Intersect

Sulukule, Istanbul · city-scale research and concept design · under the consultancy of Ozan Avcı

An urban conditioner for a neighbourhood known for its music and its market gardens. The research maps what survived the clearances, then proposes a structure along the land walls that gives dance, craft and growing a shared roof.

Argenteuil

Argenteuil, Paris region · with CBA · urban design

A former industrial riverside becomes a mixed neighbourhood where housing, work and recreation share the ground. Pedestrian routes and planted corridors tie the riverfront to the courtyards behind it, and the industrial grain of the site is kept.

Yenişehir Park housing

Mersin · architectural design · with Sara Bozkurt

The 28,313 m² site is planned by performance, not by parcel. One pedestrian spine runs through it as a planted corridor and keeps the ground car-free; the buildings take 9,578 m² of a permitted 14,156.

Toulouse Business School

Toulouse · with CBA

A school stacked on a compact footprint. The ground floor opens to the street; classrooms and a large auditorium sit above, joined by terraces where students meet without arranging to.

Housing in Hamam

Finike, Antalya · concept project · under the consultancy of Ozan Avcı

A bathhouse and places to stay, set in a disused quarry and organised by the senses. The project starts from a walked route through Finike, recording what each stop smelled, sounded and felt like, and ends in plans at five levels.

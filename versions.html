Zelka site, six versions

Same content in each, so the difference is the design. v1 to v3 use no photographs yet. v4 uses the pictures and drawings from Elif’s own portfolio files. Inside a version, the pill at the bottom or keys 0 to 5 switch.

  1. v0CurrentThe site as it was: Peristyle drawing hero, sheets on a dark desk, Wikimedia photographs.
  2. v1PracticeThe register of a working studio. White, ink, no colour in the interface. Projects written as reports with facts lists. Archivo and Fraunces.
  3. v2PublicationHer benchmark carried through: title page, room plates, numbered figures, the dark room. Archivo wide, Fraunces, Plex Mono.
  4. v395 cmOne idea leads: the Urban95 question and the scale instrument as the first screen, then a true 95 cm line. Host Grotesk.
  5. v4ScaleOne line-drawn globe that turns to each place and zooms in as you scroll: city, building, room, object, body, pixel. Her real projects and pictures. Archivo and Plex Mono.
  6. v5Blueprintv4 printed as a blueprint: white line on blue, a drawing grid and sheet border, headings that draw themselves, pictures as cyanotypes until you hover. Archivo and Courier Prime.

01

A city, a building, a room, an object, a screen. I have never experienced these as separate disciplines. They are different magnifications of the same problem.

What changes between scales is the material. What remains constant is the method: observe, question, give form. This publication documents that method through its evidence: projects spanning seven years, four countries and four orders of magnitude.

Zümrütevler Square,
Istanbul Maltepe

If you could redesign a public square from a height of 95 centimetres, you would notice different things. Broken pavements become barriers. Traffic becomes a wall of noise. A bench is unreachable. A planter is a horizon.

The team used tactical urbanism (paint, planters, flexible furniture) to test interim changes before justifying permanent reconstruction. The interim was realised in October 2019 and the permanent square completed in August 2020. Cheap. Fast. Measurable.

Type
Urban design, interim implementation and capital investment
Client
Maltepe Municipality and Bernard van Leer Foundation, Urban95
With
Superpool, with consultancy from NACTO GDCI
Size

Role
Design team, Superpool

After the interim intervention, the data showed something the team had hoped for but could not have assumed: the square was no longer a transit corridor. It had become a destination.

Tree of Life

Immersive installation · CGI, projection, narrative · Cat. 05

The client said: “I want a tree that feels alive.” That sentence was the entire brief. Everything else had to be directed.

Creative brief, first draft Superseded

“A spectacular generative tree. Maximum particle density. The audience should feel overwhelmed by the scale of the projection.”

Iteration 1 of 7 · rejected after first review

Creative brief, final approved

“The tree grows, responds, breathes light. Reduce particle density until the canopy breathes. The emotion is quiet wonder, not spectacle. The audience should feel they are inside the tree, not looking at it.”

Iteration 7 · delivered to the CGI team

Direction note, to the CGI team

“The trunk needs weight. Right now it floats. There is mass at the base of a real oak. Add 30% more branch thickness below the first fork.”

Review 4

Direction note, particle review

“The tree is suffocating. Reduce leaf particles by 40%. Let it breathe. Every particle that remains must earn its place.”

Review 7 · final adjustment before delivery

Seven iterations. Each one removed something. The final version was quieter, slower and more alive than the first. The client had asked for spectacle. What they received was presence. They approved it in silence.

# Product

Personal portfolio site for Elif Ebrar Zelka, architect, Split, Croatia.
Her line: "Works across scales". City, building, room, object and screen as one problem at different magnifications.

Written from the brief and her own visual benchmark (`zelka-benchmark-v2.html`); no interview was held, so the points marked (assumed) need her confirmation.

- **Surface mode:** Experience (a portfolio; the work and the drawing lead).
- **Visitor:** studios, clients and collaborators deciding whether to write to her (assumed).
- **One action:** write to Elif.
- **Brand, fixed by her benchmark:** paper #F4F1EA, ink #191813, red #B3341F; Archivo (wide, heavy, capitals), Fraunces (light), IBM Plex Mono only for drawing notes and data. The red hairline and mono notation are hers and stay inside drawings.
- **Voice:** an observation, not a declaration. Short sentences. Evidence over adjectives.
- **Signature:** the opening drawing: pencil sketch, crane assembly, colour rain, the pen. One authored moment; the rest of the page stays quiet.
- **Not known yet:** contact email, portrait, project photographs, case study pages.
